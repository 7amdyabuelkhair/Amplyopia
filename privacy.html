Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 18, 2026

1) Data We Collect

We collect the minimum data needed to provide the Amplyopia service, including your email address, age (or date of birth for age calculation), and in-game scores/performance data.

2) How We Use Data

We use collected data to operate and improve the platform, track game progress and score history, personalize user experience, and support reports that help users and caregivers follow improvement over time.

3) Data Protection

We apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss. No system is 100% secure, but we continuously improve protection controls.

4) No Sale of Personal Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties.

5) User Rights

You may request access to your stored data and request deletion of your data/account, subject to legal or operational requirements. To make a request, contact our support channel.

6) Children's Privacy

Amplyopia is designed for child vision training with adult supervision. If a child is below the age required by local law, parental or guardian consent is required before account creation and use.

7) Data Retention

We keep data only as long as needed for service delivery, legal obligations, and safety. If you request deletion, we will remove or anonymize data where applicable.

8) Policy Updates

We may revise this Privacy Policy over time. Material changes may require users to re-accept updated policy terms.
